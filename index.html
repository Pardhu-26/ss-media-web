Our Story

We Don't Just Record — We Create

Founded in Kakinada, SSMEDIA was born from a passion for visual storytelling. Over 20 years, we have grown from a photography duo into a full-service media production house trusted by hundreds of families, brands, and event organizers across states.

From intimate wedding ceremonies to massive corporate events with thousand-seat LED walls — every project we undertake is approached with the same commitment: cinematic quality, personal touch.

Our Services

End-to-end production capabilities, from pre-production planning to final delivery.

Video Editing

Cinematic color grading, seamless cuts, motion graphics, and professional audio sync for weddings, events, and brand films.

Audio Editing

Studio-quality sound design, background music sync, noise removal, voice-over mixing, and mastering for all productions.

Photography

Portrait, event, product, and architectural photography with professional lighting setups and post-processing finesse.

Cinematic Videography

4K wedding films, corporate videos, reels, and documentary-style coverage that tell your story beautifully.

Drone Shoots

Licensed aerial cinematography for weddings, real estate, events, and landscape documentation — sweeping shots from above.

LED Wall for Events

Massive indoor and outdoor LED wall setups for weddings, concerts, corporate launches, and political events across AP.
